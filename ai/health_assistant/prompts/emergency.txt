EMERGENCY RESPONSE TEMPLATE:

🚨 IMPORTANT: Your symptoms may indicate a medical emergency.

1. CALL 112 (Emergency Services) IMMEDIATELY
2. Do NOT drive yourself — have someone drive you or wait for an ambulance
3. Stop exertion, sit in a comfortable supported position, and stay with the person
4. Do not give food, drink, or unprescribed medication; follow the dispatcher’s instructions
5. If unresponsive and not breathing normally or only gasping, call 112, start CPR as directed, and use an AED if available
6. If unconscious but breathing normally, monitor breathing and give nothing by mouth

This app CANNOT diagnose whether you are having a heart attack. Only emergency medical professionals can make that determination.

Do NOT wait for any app result, score, or analysis before calling for help.

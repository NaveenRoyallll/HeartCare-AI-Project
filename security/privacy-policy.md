# CardioGuard AI — Privacy Policy & Notice of Health Data Practices

> [!NOTE]
> **Effective Date**: October 17, 2026  
> **Applicability**: All users of CardioGuard AI applications, mobile clients, and physician web dashboards.  
> **Compliance Alignment**: India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA 2023), ISO/IEC 27701 (Privacy Information Management), and global health data protection principles.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

CardioGuard AI processes personal and sensitive personal health data solely to deliver cardiovascular tracking, risk estimation, and emergency support.

### 1.1 Personally Identifiable Information (PII)
- Account identifiers: Full name, verified email address, contact telephone number.
- Demographic parameters: Age (years), biological sex (required strictly for epidemiological risk models).

### 1.2 Sensitive Personal Health Data (SPHD / Telemetry)
- Longitudinal biometric records: Systolic blood pressure, diastolic blood pressure, resting heart rate, blood oxygen saturation ($SpO_2$), blood glucose readings.
- Lifestyle and clinical risk factors: Smoking history, hypertension medication status, diabetes status, total cholesterol, HDL cholesterol.
- Digital signal tracings: Raw or sampled ECG voltage arrays and computed HRV metrics (research mode).
- Emergency event logs: Selected acute warning symptoms and emergency call execution timestamps.

### 1.3 Technical & Telemetry Data
- Device identifiers, app version, OS platform, and cryptographically hashed IP addresses recorded in immutable access audit trails.

---

## 2. Lawful Grounds and Purpose of Processing

We collect and process your personal and health information under the following legal bases:
1. **Explicit Patient Consent**: For processing biometric vitals, computing risk indices, and displaying trends.
2. **Vital Interests of the Data Principal**: For triggering emergency triage guidelines and notifying designated emergency contacts during suspected acute cardiovascular events.
3. **Legitimate Educational & Research Purpose**: Anonymized, non-identifiable aggregations utilized to calibrate epidemiological models (subject to explicit opt-in).

---

## 3. Data Sharing & Disclosure Principles

CardioGuard AI operates on a **zero commercial monetization of health data** policy.
- **We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal health data to third-party advertisers, data brokers, or insurance companies.**
- **Doctor Collaboration**: Your records are only visible to a healthcare provider if you explicitly approve an Access Request inside the app. You may revoke this access at any time with immediate effect.
- **Emergency Dispatch**: If you initiate an emergency contact alert, your name, selected symptoms, and device location are transmitted solely to the contacts you explicitly configured.
- **Legal Mandates**: We disclose data only where strictly mandated by applicable judicial warrant or statutory regulation.

---

## 4. Indian Data Protection Considerations (DPDPA 2023)

In accordance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023** of India:
1. **Consent Manager & Notice**: You receive clear, itemized notice in plain language regarding each category of health data processed.
2. **Right to Access & Correction**: You have the right to request a summary of your personal data and rectify inaccurate health entries.
3. **Right to Erasure & "Right to be Forgotten"**: Upon account deletion, all personal identifiers and raw biometric data are irreversibly purged or cryptographically anonymized.
4. **Data Fiduciary Accountability**: CardioGuard AI employs reasonable technical and organizational security safeguards to prevent data breaches.
5. **Grievance Redressal**: Users may reach the designated Data Protection Officer (DPO) at `dpo@cardioguard.ai` with responses mandated within 30 days.

---

## 5. Security Safeguards

- **Encryption in Transit**: TLS 1.3 with Perfect Forward Secrecy.
- **Encryption at Rest**: AES-256 encrypted database volumes and cryptographic hashing of sensitive identity tokens.
- **Access Separation**: Role-Based Access Control preventing unauthorized staff or peer access.
